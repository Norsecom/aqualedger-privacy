Privacy Policy - RIVA

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Sync Technology ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use the RIVA mobile application (the "App"). RIVA was previously published under the name AquaLedger.

RIVA lets residents view the water consumption, bills and leak alerts for their own flat or apartment. Accounts are created by your property owner or building management; the App has no self-registration.

1. Information We Collect

The App sends and receives the following information through our servers:

  • Account information: your name, email address, password, user ID and account role.
  • Residence information: your flat or apartment number, and the contact details of your property owner or manager that are shown to you in the App.
  • Water usage and billing: meter readings, consumption history, tariff rates, bills and leak alerts linked to your water meter.
  • Support requests: the subject, description and priority of any support ticket you raise in the App.
  • Device and notification information: a push-notification token, your device manufacturer and model, your operating-system version, and whether notifications are switched on. This is used only to deliver alerts to your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, videos, files, advertising identifiers, or any credit card or banking information. The App does not process payments.

2. How We Use Information

  • To sign you in and keep your session active.
  • To show your water consumption, tariff and bills, and to let you save a bill as a PDF.
  • To send you leak, billing and service notifications.
  • To answer your support tickets.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing or profiling, and we do not sell it.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not share your information with advertising networks or data brokers. Your information is handled only by:

  • Service providers working on our behalf: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers push notifications, and our cloud hosting providers, which run our servers and database.
  • Your property owner or building management: they administer your account and can see the consumption and billing data for your unit.
  • Authorities: where we are required to disclose information by law.

4. Storage and Security

  • All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS).
  • On your device, your sign-in details are kept in encrypted storage protected by the Android Keystore.
  • Bills you save as PDF are stored in the location you choose. Temporary copies kept by the App are removed when you log out.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account, consumption and billing information for as long as your account is active. When your account is closed, we delete or anonymise this information within 90 days, except for billing records that we are required by law to keep. Your push-notification token is removed from our servers when you log out of the App.

6. Deleting Your Data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information linked to it at any time. Send an email from your registered email address to support@norse-com.com with the subject "RIVA data deletion request". We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law.

7. Your Choices

  • You can turn notifications on or off in the App or in your device settings at any time.
  • You can change your password inside the App.
  • You can ask us for a copy of, or a correction to, the information we hold about you by emailing us.

8. Children

RIVA is intended for adult residents. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
