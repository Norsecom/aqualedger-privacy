Privacy Policy - RIVA

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Sync Technology ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use the RIVA mobile application (the "App").

RIVA lets residents view the water consumption, bills and leak alerts for their flat or apartment. Accounts are created by your property owner or building management.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, password, user ID and account role.
  • Residence information: your flat or apartment number.
  • Water usage and billing: meter readings, consumption history, tariff rates, bills and leak alerts linked to your water meter.
  • Support requests: the subject and description of support tickets you raise in the App.
  • Device and notification information: a push-notification token, your device model and operating-system version, used to deliver alerts to your device.
  • Crash information: if the App crashes, a crash report containing technical details such as device model, operating-system version, app version and an installation identifier.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, videos, advertising identifiers, or any card or banking information. The App does not process payments.

2. How We Use Information

  • To sign you in and show your water consumption, tariff and bills.
  • To send you leak, billing and service notifications.
  • To respond to your support tickets.
  • To find and fix crashes and keep the App working reliably.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Sharing

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers. It is handled only by:

  • Service providers working on our behalf: Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging for push notifications and Crashlytics for crash reports) and our cloud hosting providers.
  • Your property owner or building management: they administer your account and can see the consumption and billing data for your unit.
  • Authorities: where disclosure is required by law.

4. Security

All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS). Sign-in details kept on your device are stored in encrypted storage protected by the Android Keystore.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When your account is closed, we delete or anonymise it within 90 days, except for billing records we are required by law to keep.

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information linked to it at any time. Send an email from your registered email address to support@norse-com.com with the subject "RIVA data deletion request". We will complete the request within 30 days.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
