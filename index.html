Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to AquaLedger. This Privacy Policy explains how NORSECOM ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the AquaLedger mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection

AquaLedger is designed with minimalism and user privacy as core principles. The App is strictly used for displaying water consumption metrics and generating utility bills.

  • No Personal Data Harvesting: We do not track, collect, or sell your personal device data, location data, or contacts.
  • No Payment Gateways: The App currently does not process live payments. We do not collect or store credit card or banking information.
  • Meter Data: The App connects to our secure servers solely to retrieve and display your designated water meter readings and billing statements.

2. Data Usage

Any data retrieved by the App is used strictly for the purpose of rendering your utility dashboard and calculating consumption metrics. We do not use this data for advertising or marketing.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not share your water consumption data with third-party advertising networks or data brokers. The App only communicates with our official secure backend infrastructure.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
